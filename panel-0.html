Start with who you are—not a logo.

This session is about your personal brand: how you want to show up, what you want people to know and feel about you, and what you want to be remembered for. A logo or visual style can support that later, but it is not the end result.

“Your brand is not a costume. It is the clearest, most intentional version of what is already true about you.”
First: notice.
What matters to you? What do people trust you for? When do you feel most like yourself?
Then: choose.
Decide what you want to be known for and how you want people to feel when they experience you or your work.
Finally: express.
Use your words, actions, stories, visuals, and creative choices to reinforce that intentionally.
What you will leave with: a personalized Personal Brand Kit that captures how you want to show up, who you are here for, what you stand for, how you naturally communicate, what you want to be known for, and how to express it consistently. You are not building a logo here.

One thing to know about AI before you begin

Think of AI a little like the ocean right now: powerful, wide open, and still being charted. You are navigating one of the first generations of this experience, so it will not get everything perfect on the first try. That is normal.

Use AI to explore, draft, compare, organize, and refine. Later, you can use tools like Canva, Google Slides, or Microsoft to express your choices visually. You are still the navigator and the creative director. You decide what feels true, what stays, and what changes.

If something does not feel like you, it is not finished yet.

You cannot get this wrong. You are building a first version, not carving your identity into stone. A personal brand can evolve as you do.
