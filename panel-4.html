Choose Your Look

A visual brand is not “what colors do I like?” It is “what should people feel before they read a word?”

Use feeling words before style words.

Think spaces, magazines, clothing, packaging, websites, creators, photography, art, or places—not only logos.

A simple visual recipe
Colors
Start with 1 main color, 1 dark neutral, 1 light neutral, and 1 accent. Add more only if you need them.
Fonts
Start with one headline font and one easy-to-read body font. An optional accent font can be used sparingly.
Images
Pick one consistent photography or illustration feeling: candid, editorial, bright, soft, bold, natural, polished, etc.
Mood board tip: Save 12–20 images that create the feeling you want. Do not ask “does this match my business?” at first. Ask “does this feel like my world?” Then look for repeated colors, textures, lighting, shapes, clothing, spaces, and energy.
