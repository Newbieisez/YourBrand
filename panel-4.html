Choose Your Visual Direction

This is one expression of your personal brand—not the brand itself. Decide how you want your presence to feel visually before you open any design tool.

Your visuals support the impression you want to create. They can make your personal brand easier to recognize, but they do not define who you are. If you never create a logo, you can still have a strong personal brand.

Use feeling words before style words.

Think spaces, magazines, clothing, packaging, websites, creators, photography, art, or places—not only logos.

Choose your core colors

Keep this simple. Start with one main color, one dark neutral, one light neutral, and one accent. You can refine them later.

Add the HEX codes if you know them. If you do not, write the color names for now.

Decide what is used for text, backgrounds, highlights, and accents.

Choose your font direction

You only need two: one for headlines and one that is easy to read for everything else.

Choose your image direction
Photography
Candid, editorial, natural light, polished, bright, moody, documentary?
Texture
Clean, soft, tactile, bold, minimal, layered, organic?
People + places
What kinds of people, environments, clothing, objects, and energy belong in your world?
Mood board tip: Save 12–20 images that create the feeling you want. Look for repeated colors, textures, lighting, shapes, clothing, spaces, and energy.
Do not open Canva yet. First make the choices. The tool comes later. Otherwise it is too easy to let templates decide how you should present yourself.
