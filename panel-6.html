Shape Your Message

Give people a simple way to understand what you do, why it matters, and what they can expect from you.

Try: “I help [who] do [outcome] through [approach].” It does not need to be clever.

Choose three themes you want people to connect with your name or business. These will guide what you talk about most often.

Clarity beats cleverness. If someone understands what you do but cannot repeat your tagline, that is better than remembering a beautiful tagline and having no idea what you offer.
