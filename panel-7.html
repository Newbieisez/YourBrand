Create With AI Without Losing Yourself

AI becomes much more useful when you give it something real to work with: your beliefs, examples, audience, facts, and natural voice.

You can steer the result

AI may give you something too polished, too generic, too cheerful, too corporate, or simply not quite right. That is not a failure. Tell it what feels wrong in your own words: “This sounds too formal,” “I would never say that,” “Make this warmer,” or “Keep the idea but make it sound more like me.”

Treat the first answer like a first draft, not a verdict.

Give AI ingredients.
Your brand guide + the audience + the goal + real source material.
Ask for help, not identity.
Use AI to organize, draft, challenge, shorten, repurpose, and explore—not to decide what you believe.

Paste notes, a transcript, product details, customer feedback, research, or your rough thoughts.

Here is a ready-to-send message based on your brand. Copy it into the AI tool you like to use, then keep shaping the answer until it feels right.

Before you publish: read the result out loud. Ask: “Would I actually say this?” “Is every factual claim true?” “Does this make my audience feel the way I want them to feel?” If not, revise it.
