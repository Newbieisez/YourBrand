Find Your Voice

Your brand voice should sound like a clearer version of you, not like a business textbook or a robot.

The easiest place to start: collect things you have already written or said when you felt natural. Texts to a friend, emails you were proud of, captions, meeting transcripts, voice notes, presentations, or client messages all count.

They do not need to be polished. Real is more useful than impressive.

Short or detailed? Playful or calm? Direct or gentle? Story-driven? Data-driven? Do you ask questions? Use humor? Explain with examples?

Try the “friend test”
Your voice can have range. You can be warm in a welcome email, firm in a boundary, playful on social media, and precise in a proposal. Consistency does not mean sounding identical everywhere.

If you want another set of eyes, copy this into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or the AI tool you already use. It asks the AI to notice patterns in your real writing—not invent a personality for you.
